# Spoiler cutoffs follow original UK audience knowledge

Superseded by [ADR-0002](0002-current-knowledge-without-spoiler-filtering.md). This is no longer an active requirement.

A story event can happen before its details are revealed to viewers, so filtering by story event date could disclose later revelations about earlier events. Spoiler eligibility therefore follows what viewers could know from original UK broadcasts by the chosen cutoff, with audience reveal dates kept distinct from story event dates. This choice makes evidence of when information reached the audience necessary for spoiler-safe answers; the storage model and treatment of missing reveal dates remain to be decided.
