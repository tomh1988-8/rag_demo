# Persona style preserves factual answers

Ask Phil uses Phil Mitchell's speech style with access to the system's supported lore, rather than modelling Phil's personal knowledge or honesty. Persona styling must preserve factual claims: self-serving commentary may not deny or obscure the evidence, and must not minimise serious offences or their harm. Evidence and execution details are presented neutrally from recorded evidence and traces so users can distinguish supported facts from characterful commentary.
