# Initial character roster

The initial corpus covers 20 curated core characters chosen for prominence, connected stories, coverage across eras, and available source material. This is a selected demo roster, not a definitive popularity ranking.

## Scope

- A core character qualifies by appearing in the main EastEnders television series during or after 1990, even if their debut was earlier.
- Retain relevant earlier story history so relationships and events remain understandable.
- Exclude spin-off and podcast material from the initial corpus, including when it appears on the same source page as main-series material.
- Retain evidenced supporting characters and connections outside the core 20. Their own histories may have only partial coverage.
- Source-page availability does not establish complete character coverage or independent verification of its claims.
- Expand the core roster through the same process, with eventual coverage of most notable characters.
