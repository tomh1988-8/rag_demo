# Source review before extraction

The twelve annotations in `data/references/relationships-v1.json` were reviewed
against the unchanged captured `core-profiles-v2` source documents before
extending extraction. Ten selected excerpts retain their original revision,
capture metadata, document offsets and source identity. The source corpus and
earlier references are unchanged. This is an exposed development slice reviewed
by an agent, without human adjudication or independent canon corroboration.

Phil and Kathy marry, separate when Kathy moves out, and reconcile when Phil
attends counselling. These are three assertions in one marriage episode;
neither separation nor reconciliation proves divorce or remarriage. Their
calendar dates are unknown. The relative counselling anchor is retained.

Phil and Sharon's affair and Sharon's reconciliation with Grant are separate
relationships. Grant and Sharon's later divorce is explicitly narrated in a
contiguous excerpt crossing two retained chunks of the same paragraph. The
paragraph mentions an unfulfilled proposal: that is not a marriage. No ordering
link to the other selected paragraph is labelled merely from document order.

Kat and Alfie's Christmas Day marriage has no stated year. A separate excerpt
explicitly says they remarry on leaving hospital. This is a second marriage
episode, following their first; its calendar date is unknown. It must not be
collapsed into the first marriage or confused with a reconciliation.

Den's adoption of Sharon at age three, Denise's explicitly named stepdaughter
Amy in early 2023, and Phil/Kathy's biological parentage of Ben in March 1996
retain distinct directed meanings. Ward/guardian distinctions and current/former
statuses receive separately labelled synthetic counterexamples. Marriage alone
does not establish a step-parent, and a ward does not establish adoption.

Amy's name and performer Ellie Dadd are explicit in the captured Denise text.
Her supporting identity uses Wikidata Q136428618, as listed by the
[Wikidata EastEnders character project](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:WikiProject_EastEnders/Characters).
That metadata lookup establishes an identifier, not independent story support.
The other nine identities reuse the reviewed captured catalogue unchanged.

Matching ignores arbitrary claim/episode names, normalizes symmetric romantic
participants, preserves social direction and checks episode membership and
ordering separately. Explicit acceptable timing paraphrases are frozen with the
reference. Evidence resolution and conservative full-excerpt grounding are
separate from value accuracy. Reference labels will not be adjusted to fit the
pilot output. No broad or held-out extraction quality is claimed.
